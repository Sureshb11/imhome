Alert Escalation

The right response for every situation

Not every alert is an emergency. Our tiered system ensures you get the appropriate level of care — never too little, never too much.

Level 1 — Informational

App Notification

Mild deviations from baseline — slightly elevated resting heart rate, missed step goal, or minor sleep disruption. Logged for your doctor's review.

Level 2 — Advisory

Clinical Team Contact

BP above threshold, irregular ECG pattern, or sustained SpO₂ dip. A registered nurse calls you within minutes to assess and advise.

Level 3 — Urgent

Emergency Protocol

Critical vitals or patient-initiated SOS. Immediate clinical team response, physician notification, and emergency services coordination if needed.
