Program Details

What's included in each specialty

🩺 Diabetes Care

Comprehensive type 1 and type 2 diabetes management with continuous glucose integration and lifestyle coaching.

  • CGM data synced to your care dashboard
  • Doctor-set daily walking targets
  • HbA1c trend tracking and lab integration
  • Registered dietitian video sessions

❤️ Cardiac Monitoring

Continuous cardiac surveillance for hypertension, arrhythmia, and post-discharge recovery.

  • Single-lead ECG patch with 21 arrhythmia detection
  • Cuffless or cuff-based BP monitoring
  • SpO₂ and heart rate variability tracking
  • Cardiologist video consultations

🫁 Pulmonology

Respiratory monitoring for COPD, asthma, and post-COVID lung recovery patients.

  • Continuous SpO₂ and respiratory rate
  • Activity and sleep correlation analysis
  • Exacerbation early-warning alerts
  • Pulmonologist video follow-ups

🧴 Dermatology

Remote skin assessments without leaving home — ideal for chronic conditions and post-treatment follow-up.

  • Secure photo upload and dermatologist review
  • Treatment plan adjustments via video
  • Medication adherence tracking
  • Scheduled follow-up consultations

🥗 Nutrition

Personalized nutrition coaching integrated with your biosensor and glucose data.

  • Registered dietitian video sessions
  • Glucose-aware meal planning
  • Macro and calorie goal tracking
  • Progress reports shared with your physician

🧠 Mental Health

Behavioral health support with physiological stress insights from HRV and sleep data.

  • Licensed psychologist video therapy
  • HRV-based stress and recovery scoring
  • Sleep quality correlation with mood
  • Confidential, HIPAA-compliant sessions

🦴 Orthopedics

Post-surgical and chronic joint recovery with mobility and activity monitoring.

  • Daily step and mobility tracking
  • Posture and movement analysis
  • Recovery milestone reporting to surgeon
  • Physical therapy coordination via video
