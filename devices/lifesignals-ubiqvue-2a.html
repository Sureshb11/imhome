UbiqVue 2A Multiparameter Biosensor

Leaders in wireless healthcare technologies — purpose-built silicon for untethered monitoring.

FDA 510(k) Hospital & At-Home

A single-use wireless multiparameter biosensor designed for hospital and at-home continuous monitoring, with SpO₂, ECG, and near real-time cloud reporting.

What It Measures

  • 2-channel ECG
  • SpO₂
  • Heart Rate & HRV
  • Respiration Rate
  • Body & Skin Temperature
  • Posture & Motion

Key Specifications

  • World's first single-use wireless biosensor with SpO₂
  • 12 monitored parameters in near real-time
  • 116×91×17 mm, 35g — up to 5-day wear
  • Single-use, IP24, defibrillation-proof
  • WLAN + BLE 5.2 connectivity
  • UbiqVue cloud web portal & BioWeb

Platform & Clearance

  • FDA 510(k) cleared UbiqVue 2A system
  • UbiqVue cloud web portal
  • Hospital & at-home patient care settings
  • Sold to hospitals & OEM partners
